🌿 Explore Cross River

Discover the beauty of Cross River State, Nigeria

Website Planning Document

1. Site Name

Explore Cross River

Explore Cross River is the name of a tourism website dedicated to showcasing the natural beauty, culture, and tourist attractions of Cross River State, Nigeria. I chose this name because it is simple, memorable, and encourages people to discover interesting places in the state.

Proposed domain: explorecrossriver.com

🌿 Explore Cross River

The logo uses a green nature symbol to represent the beautiful forests, waterfalls, and natural environment of Cross River State.

2. Site Purpose

The purpose of Explore Cross River is to provide visitors with useful information about tourist attractions in Cross River State, Nigeria. The website will introduce popular destinations, describe their locations, showcase photographs, and provide information that can help people plan their visits.

The website will feature places such as Obudu Mountain Resort, Agbokim Waterfalls, Kwa Falls, and Cross River National Park. It will also provide travel information and contact details for people who need more information.

3. Scenarios

These are questions that visitors to the website may ask:

  • What are the most popular tourist attractions to visit in Cross River State?
  • Where is Obudu Mountain Resort located, and what can visitors enjoy there?
  • How can I find information about waterfalls and nature destinations in Cross River?
  • Where can I get information to help me plan a visit to these attractions?

4. Color Schema

The website will use natural colors inspired by the forests, water, and environment of Cross River State.

Forest Green – #245C3A

Used for headings, navigation, and important accents.

Warm Cream – #F5F1E8

Used for the main page background and content sections.

River Blue – #267A91

Used for links, buttons, and secondary accents.

Dark Gray – #292929

Used for paragraph text to provide good readability.

5. Typography

The website will use two system fonts to make the content attractive and easy to read.

Georgia – Headings

Georgia will be used for the main title, section headings, and website logo because it gives the website a clear and attractive appearance.

Arial – Body Text

Arial will be used for paragraphs, navigation, descriptions, and general website content because it is simple and easy to read.

7. Project Development Plan

I plan to develop the website using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. HTML will organize the website content, CSS will control its appearance and responsive layout, and JavaScript will provide interactive features where necessary.

The website will be designed to work on mobile phones, tablets, and desktop computers. I will also use optimized images, meaningful alternative text, and accessible color combinations to improve the visitor experience.
